# Final Exam

I want you to notice something. 

Look back at everything you've deposited into Github. All of that work has been merely graded as done/not done. This is explicitly by design: I just want you to try these things without fear of the 'right' answer. If you've been doing this in good faith, you should see an evolution in your thinking as you go through your materials. There will be things that worked very easily, and things that were hard. Sometimes, the easiest things might be the most troubling, when you think about them in the context of the readings and my lectures; sometimes the technically hardest things might not have shifted your understanding of the archaeological materials too much, but maybe they changed how you _think_ about the process. We don't have an archaeology degree here; you are all coming from programs in History or the Humanities or elsewhere. How has your thinking about your 'home' discipline changed by this encounter with mine?

It's this process and evidence of your own growth that I want you to notice.

During the exams, I will be asking you two questions that draw that out more.

1. Discuss, with explicit reference to the readings and your own research compendia, the evolution in your thinking about digital archaeology over the course of this term. Frame your response in terms of what this course has taught you about yourself as a researcher, as a scholar. Do this in the light of three significant 'fails' you encountered in this course.
2. Reflect on the how digital archaeology creates new knowledge by telling me about the piece of work of which you are most proud, or the piece of work that sticks with you even now (think about the learning outcomes in this course). Contextualize the work for me and why it matters by explicit reference to the readings, lectures, exercises, conversations you've had in this course and the feedback you received from me with regard to the first and second consolidation pieces. 

You'll have the full 3 hours to answer these two questions fully and completely.
